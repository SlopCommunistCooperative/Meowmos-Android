* Material 3 Expressive adds spring animations and a new loading indicator.
* App, widget, and shortcut icons use Material Symbols Sharp.
* Note and archive menus, the note editor, and comment controls use Material 3 Expressive.
* Tabs fade without reloading cached notes. Home shows placeholders while an empty feed loads.
